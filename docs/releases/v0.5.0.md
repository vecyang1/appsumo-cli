# v0.5.0 — the weekly buy advisor

Released 2026-10-07. Not yet tagged.

A scheduled advisor that audits each week's AppSumo candidates against the
lifetime assets you already own, and recommends buying nothing when every deal
is a duplicate.

## Added

- **Weekly Buy Advisor engine** — `scripts/appsumo_buy_advisor.py` and the runner
  wrapper `scripts/run_appsumo_buy_advisor.sh` audit candidate deals against
  65+ lifetime purchased assets.
- **Zero-recommendation invariant and anti-duplicate guards** — word-boundary
  token matching, 40+ competitor `alternative_to` mappings (for example
  Zapier/Make → n8n, Trustpilot → TrustMate, Buffer → Zernio), saturated
  category guards (Reviews, Popups, Scheduling, Forms, Social), Google Sheets AI
  add-ons, WooCommerce builders, client portals, dev tools, and `$0` /
  non-software item filters. If every deal is a duplicate or low leverage, the
  advisor emits zero recommendations.
- **Unit test suite** — `scripts/test_appsumo_buy_advisor.py` (13/13 passing)
  covering exact matches, fuzzy/token normalization, `alternative_to` aliases,
  saturated subcategories, free/low-value filters, and zero recommendations.
- **Weekly cadence card** — `CAD-20261007-appsumo-weekly-buy-advisor` (runs every
  Monday at 09:00), registered across the 2nd Brain cadence registry, local
  documentation, and the launchd sidecar.
- **Notion receipts** — automated sync of advisory receipts to the Notion
  Goal/Task pages.
